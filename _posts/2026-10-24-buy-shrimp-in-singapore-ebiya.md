This page explains how buying works at ebiya.sg, from the first message to collection. We breed Caridina shrimp in Singapore, and we also sell Neocaridina.

## What it costs

Prices are per piece, and the price varies by individual shrimp. There is no minimum order. We are not GST-registered, so our prices have no GST.

From the prices on our [shrimp page](/shrimp/):

- [Fancy red tigers](/shrimp/fancy-red-tigers/): from S$60 each
- [Blue king kong](/shrimp/blue-king-kong/): from S$40 each
- White spectra: from S$60 each
- Orange boa: from S$60 each

For [Neocaridina](/shrimp/neocaridina/) and [CRS](/shrimp/crs/), message us for the price. [Red devils](/shrimp/red-devils/) are not for sale yet.

## How to book

1. Message us on WhatsApp at [+65 6042 5590](https://wa.me/6560425590) and say which shrimp you want.
2. We confirm what is available. The numbers on the [shrimp page](/shrimp/) show how many we have of each type.
3. We agree a time for you to collect.

## Collection and payment

Collection is at 55 Changi Road, #B1-01, Singapore 419709, on Tuesday from 1pm to 6pm, by appointment. Please message us first. Payment is on collection, by PayNow or cash.
