ebiya.sg is a shrimp shop in Singapore that breeds its own Caridina shrimp (*Caridina cantonensis*) and also sells Neocaridina. This page explains how buying works, from the first message to collection.

## Quick answers

- **Where is the shop?** 55 Changi Road, #B1-01, Singapore 419709.
- **When can I collect?** Tuesday, 1pm to 6pm, by appointment. Message us first.
- **How do I book?** WhatsApp [+65 6042 5590](https://wa.me/6560425590).
- **How much are the shrimp?** Prices are per piece and vary by individual shrimp. Fancy red tigers from S$60, blue king kong from S$40.
- **Is there a minimum order?** No.
- **How do I pay?** On collection, by PayNow or cash. We are not GST-registered, so prices have no GST.
- **What sizes do you sell?** Juveniles (less than 1 cm) and young adults (about 1 cm or more).
