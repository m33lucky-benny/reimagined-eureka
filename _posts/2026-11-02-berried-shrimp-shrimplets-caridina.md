A **berried** shrimp is a female carrying eggs. When you visit our tanks, you will often see berried females and, around them, tiny shrimplets.

## What "berried" means

Shrimp Science explains that a female carrying eggs is called a berried shrimp. She holds the eggs under her body, on her swimmerets, and carries them for around 3 to 4 weeks until they hatch ([Shrimp Science](https://www.shrimpscience.com/articles/breeding-cherry-crystal-bee-shrimp/)).

## Shrimplets

Shrimplets are the baby shrimp, and our breeding tanks have them. We do not count them. Our [fancy red tigers](/shrimp/fancy-red-tigers/) and [blue king kong](/shrimp/blue-king-kong/) breed better than our boas.

Shrimplets do not always look like their parents. Our [red devils](/shrimp/red-devils/) came out of our fancy red tiger tank. Why that happens is in [one tank, many looks](/one-tank-many-looks-caridina/).
